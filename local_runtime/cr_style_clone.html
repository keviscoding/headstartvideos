01 / CHOOSE A REFERENCE

Which channel inspires you?

Paste a public YouTube video or channel link. A channel preview lists up to eight recent videos (or Shorts) so you can choose what to study. To mix channels into one style of your own, add up to three links.

Previewing is free: it only reads the channels’ video lists. Nothing runs on your Claude account yet.

Choose the videos to study

Select up to three videos, ideally on different topics: Studio learns what stays the same across them (the channel's style) rather than one video's own setting. You're mixing channels: pick videos from each, and Studio learns one style from all of them. Say what to take from each in your brief.

Free: Studio measures a few minutes of each video on your computer and ChannelRecipe's Gemini watches them. Nothing runs on your Claude account and nothing is charged.

    Improve this style

    Say one change in your own words, for example “rounder heads”, “calmer camera moves” or “the captions in yellow”. Your Claude Code rewrites this style’s animation code, and Studio draws a few seconds of the new version on this computer, next to the current one, and checks it against the channel. Nothing changes until you save it. Usually 3 to 10 minutes of your Claude usage (at most 25), no money.

    What runs where

    Your computer downloads a few minutes of each reference, measures it and draws the test shots. ChannelRecipe's Gemini watches the videos straight from YouTube, included in your plan. Claude does the creative study on your Claude plan. Your files stay on your computer.

    Studio only learns from frames it actually watched. If a video can’t be watched or Claude can’t finish, it stops and keeps what it learned so you can review it.
