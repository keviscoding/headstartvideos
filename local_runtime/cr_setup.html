01 / CHECK YOUR COMPUTER

Make sure the tools are ready

Checking your setup…

A tool is missing. How do I fix it?

Open this downloaded folder in Claude Code and ask: “Help me complete the ChannelRecipe setup checks.” It will guide you through the missing free tools.

On Mac with Homebrew: brew install ffmpeg node. On Windows: install FFmpeg and Node, then close and reopen the launcher. Your system PATH needs to include FFmpeg.

02 / CONNECT CHANNELRECIPE

Give your studio access to CR

Copy the CR connect key from your Local studio page. Paste it here. It stays in this folder with private file permissions.

03 / ADD YOUR MEDIA ACCOUNTS

Choose the accounts your video needs

Start with voice and images. Add other services when a style needs them. These keys are saved locally and do not get sent to CR.

Saved keys appear in the check above. Saving checks the format; the provider verifies the key when used.

04 / MAKE SOMETHING

Your studio is ready when the checks pass

Choose a style, title and duration. Begin with a short video to check your output.

Open studio →

Keep the launcher's window open. Videos and saved work are in your local output folder. Return to this page using “Setup & accounts” in the studio.
