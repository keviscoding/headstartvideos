What the course covers

Three parts, one video at the end

1 · Set up

Download Studio, open it in Claude Code, paste the install prompt, connect your account, Claude and your Kie.ai key.

2 · Clone a style

Pick a channel, let Studio look at its videos, then Claude writes that channel's look as your own style, checked against its real frames.

3 · Your first video

Use the style, set the length, press Create video, then watch the result and improve the style one change at a time.

What you need

  • A ChannelRecipe plan (Studio comes with it).
  • The Claude desktop app with a Claude Pro or Max plan: Claude writes, directs and checks your videos.
  • A Kie.ai account for the voice and pictures (or your own ElevenLabs account for the voice).
