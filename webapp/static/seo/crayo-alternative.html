Which one is for you

Pick the tool that fits your channel

If you already have long videos or streams, Crayo cuts them up. If you need the long videos, that's ChannelRecipe.

Pick Crayo if

  • You stream or already have long videos and want clips cut from them.
  • You want fake-text, Reddit-story or gameplay split-screen Shorts.
  • You only post to TikTok, Reels and Shorts.

Pick ChannelRecipe if

  • You want long faceless YouTube videos that earn AdSense.
  • You want visuals made for your topic, not gameplay in the background.
  • You want one recognisable style across your channel.
  • You'd rather not be capped by export hours.

Why creators switch

Clips need a video to come from

Make the source, not just the cut

A clipping tool needs long videos to work on. Studio makes those long videos from a title, then makes Shorts as well.

Visuals that match the story

Gameplay backgrounds work for Reddit stories. A history, science or money channel needs scenes about the topic, which is what every Studio recipe draws.

Long videos pay more

Shorts build an audience but pay a small fraction per view. Long videos earn from AdSense for months after you post them.

No export-hour cap

Crayo's plans meter export hours and AI voice minutes. Studio renders on your own computer, with no per-video credits.

Questions

Crayo alternative: FAQ

Is ChannelRecipe a good Crayo alternative?
Yes, if you want long YouTube videos. Crayo is a clipping and short-form tool that works on vertical clips. ChannelRecipe makes whole long videos and 15 to 60 second Shorts from a title.
How much does Crayo cost?
On 26 November 2026 Crayo's pricing page listed Hobby at $29 a month, Clipper at $59 and Pro at $129, or $19, $39 and $79 a month billed every three months. Its FAQ says it does not offer refunds.
Does Crayo make long YouTube videos?
Its pricing and product pages describe short vertical clips for TikTok, Reels and YouTube Shorts. We found no long-form 16:9 video creation on its site.
How much does ChannelRecipe cost?
Creator is $49 a month, Pro $79 and Max $119, and yearly plans save two months. Studio also uses your own Claude Pro or Max account and a Kie.ai or Atlas Cloud balance for voice and images, which you pay those providers directly with no markup. There are no per-video credits for Studio videos.
Can ChannelRecipe make Shorts too?
Yes. Studio has three Shorts recipes: 3D Explainer Shorts, 3D Dark Stories and Photo Comedy Shorts. Type a topic, choose 15 to 60 seconds, and Studio fact-checks the script, directs every shot and cuts a vertical 9:16 video.
