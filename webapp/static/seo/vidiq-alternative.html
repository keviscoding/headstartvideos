Which one is for you

Pick one, or use both

vidIQ is strong at finding ideas and optimising them. It doesn't make long videos.

Keep vidIQ for

  • Keyword and outlier research to choose your next title.
  • Titles, tags, descriptions and thumbnail testing.
  • Channel analytics and coaching.

Use ChannelRecipe for

  • Turning that title into a finished long video.
  • Shorts from the same topics.
  • Keeping one recognisable style across every upload.
  • Facts checked before the voice is recorded.

Questions

vidIQ alternative: FAQ

Is ChannelRecipe a vidIQ alternative?
For making videos, yes. vidIQ's Video Generator makes clips of 1 to 15 seconds that can't be edited, so long videos need another tool. ChannelRecipe makes whole long videos and Shorts. For keyword research and analytics, vidIQ does things ChannelRecipe doesn't, and many creators use both.
How much does vidIQ cost?
On 26 November 2026 vidIQ had a free plan, Boost at $199 a year and MAX at $468 a year, with monthly AI credits that don't roll over.
Does ChannelRecipe help me choose topics?
Yes. Every plan includes the Niche Finder and a niche database you can use in Claude.
How much does ChannelRecipe cost?
Creator is $49 a month, Pro $79 and Max $119, and yearly plans save two months. Studio also uses your own Claude Pro or Max account and a Kie.ai or Atlas Cloud balance for voice and images, which you pay those providers directly with no markup. There are no per-video credits for Studio videos.
Can ChannelRecipe make Shorts too?
Yes. Studio has three Shorts recipes: 3D Explainer Shorts, 3D Dark Stories and Photo Comedy Shorts. Type a topic, choose 15 to 60 seconds, and Studio fact-checks the script, directs every shot and cuts a vertical 9:16 video.
