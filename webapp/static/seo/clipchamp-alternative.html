Which one is for you

Pick the tool that fits how you work

If you enjoy editing, Clipchamp is hard to beat for free. If you want the video made, that's ChannelRecipe.

Pick Clipchamp if

  • You like editing and want full manual control of every cut.
  • You need a free editor with 1080p export and no watermark.
  • You already pay for Microsoft 365.

Pick ChannelRecipe if

  • You want to post several long videos a week without editing them.
  • You want animated scenes made for your story, not stock clips.
  • You want the script written and fact-checked for you.
  • You run more than one channel.

Questions

Clipchamp alternative: FAQ

Is ChannelRecipe a good Clipchamp alternative?
Yes, if you want videos made rather than edited by hand. Clipchamp is a manual timeline editor. ChannelRecipe writes, voices, animates and edits whole faceless YouTube videos from a title.
Is Clipchamp free?
Yes. On 26 November 2026 Clipchamp's free plan let you export as many videos as you like in 1080p without a watermark. Premium, with 4K and premium stock, comes with Microsoft 365 Personal or Family.
Can I edit ChannelRecipe videos afterwards?
Yes. Review and change every scene on Studio's timeline, or export and finish the video in any editor.
How much does ChannelRecipe cost?
Creator is $49 a month, Pro $79 and Max $119, and yearly plans save two months. Studio also uses your own Claude Pro or Max account and a Kie.ai or Atlas Cloud balance for voice and images, which you pay those providers directly with no markup. There are no per-video credits for Studio videos.
Can ChannelRecipe make Shorts too?
Yes. Studio has three Shorts recipes: 3D Explainer Shorts, 3D Dark Stories and Photo Comedy Shorts. Type a topic, choose 15 to 60 seconds, and Studio fact-checks the script, directs every shot and cuts a vertical 9:16 video.
