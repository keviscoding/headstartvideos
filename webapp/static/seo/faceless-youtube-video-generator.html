Questions

Faceless YouTube video generator: FAQ

Can a faceless YouTube channel make money?
Yes. Faceless channels earn from AdSense, sponsors and affiliate links like any other channel. They have to follow YouTube's rules on original, non-repetitive content, which is why Studio writes a new researched script and a new edit for every video.
Will YouTube demonetise AI-made videos?
YouTube demonetises mass-produced and repetitive content, not AI as such. Videos with original scripts, real research and a distinct edit can be monetised. Label realistic AI-generated people or events where YouTube's disclosure rules ask you to.
What do I need to run ChannelRecipe Studio?
A Mac or Windows computer with 8 GB of memory or more (16 GB for long or 3D videos), a Claude Pro or Max account for the writing and directing, and a Kie.ai or Atlas Cloud account for voice and images. Setup checks everything and tells you what's missing.
Can I use my own script or voiceover?
Yes. Edit the AI-generated script freely, paste your own, or upload your own voiceover recording, and Studio builds the video around it.
Do I need editing skills?
No. You type a title and Studio makes the video. You can review every scene on the timeline before you export.
