Which one is for you

Pick the tool that fits your channel

They do different jobs. Here's when each one is the right call.

Pick Viblo if

  • You only want Shorts, Reels or TikToks and won't post long videos.
  • You want the lowest monthly price with nothing else to set up.
  • You'd rather work in a browser than install an app.

Pick ChannelRecipe if

  • You want long videos that earn real AdSense, with Shorts alongside.
  • You want your channel to have its own look, or the look of a channel you admire.
  • You make history, true crime, science or money videos where getting facts right matters.
  • You don't want a monthly cap on how many videos you make.

Why creators switch

From clips to a channel that earns

Long videos pay more

A faceless channel earns from AdSense on long videos. Shorts build an audience but pay a small fraction per view. Two strong 12-minute videos a week can out-earn a Short every day.

A look viewers recognise

Template clips look the same on every account that uses them. Studio gives your channel one style across every video, or learns the look of a channel you point it at.

Facts checked first

History and true crime channels lose viewers to "that date is wrong" comments. Studio researches and checks the script's facts before a word is recorded.

No video cap

Viblo's plans stop at 30, 90 or 200 Shorts a month. Studio renders on your own computer, so there are no per-video credits to run out of.
