Which one is for you

Pick the tool that fits your goal

Chasing a trend this week and building a channel that earns every month are different jobs.

Pick Everygen if

  • You want to jump on a trending format today with a ready-made preset.
  • You post mostly to Instagram and TikTok.
  • You want to try many AI video models in one place.

Pick ChannelRecipe if

  • You're building a YouTube channel that earns from long videos.
  • You want viewers to recognise your videos in the feed.
  • You want depth: history, true crime, science or money stories people watch for 15 minutes.
  • You want Shorts that send viewers to those long videos.
