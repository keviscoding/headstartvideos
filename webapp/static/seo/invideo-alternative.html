Which one is for you

Pick the tool that fits your work

A general video editor and a faceless-channel studio are different tools.

Pick InVideo if

  • You make many kinds of video, not just YouTube.
  • You want a huge stock library and many generative models in one editor.
  • You work in a team and want per-seat plans.

Pick ChannelRecipe if

  • You run faceless YouTube channels and want the whole video made.
  • You want a look built for your niche, not stock clips viewers have seen before.
  • You'd rather not count credits per video.
  • You want facts checked before the voice is recorded.

Questions

InVideo alternative: FAQ

Is ChannelRecipe a good InVideo AI alternative?
Yes, if you mainly make faceless YouTube videos. InVideo is a general AI video editor. ChannelRecipe is built to make whole faceless YouTube videos and Shorts in a consistent channel style.
How much does InVideo cost?
On 26 November 2026 InVideo's pricing page showed Starter at $20, Plus at $60 and Max at $150 per seat a month, or $10, $24 and $60 per seat a month billed yearly, with 400, 2,000 and 5,000 credits per seat. Unused credits don't roll over.
Does ChannelRecipe use stock footage?
Most Studio recipes draw scenes for your story instead, such as 3D reconstructions, stick figures and paintings, so your videos don't look like other stock-footage channels.
How much does ChannelRecipe cost?
Creator is $49 a month, Pro $79 and Max $119, and yearly plans save two months. Studio also uses your own Claude Pro or Max account and a Kie.ai or Atlas Cloud balance for voice and images, which you pay those providers directly with no markup. There are no per-video credits for Studio videos.
Can ChannelRecipe make Shorts too?
Yes. Studio has three Shorts recipes: 3D Explainer Shorts, 3D Dark Stories and Photo Comedy Shorts. Type a topic, choose 15 to 60 seconds, and Studio fact-checks the script, directs every shot and cuts a vertical 9:16 video.
