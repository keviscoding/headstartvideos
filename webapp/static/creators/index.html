      Questions, answered

      Will it cut off my words, or sound like AI?

      No. We cut by what you said, not by silence, so words stay whole and the pauses that land a line stay in. Before you see an edit, we watch and listen to it next to your take.

      Do I need to know how to edit?

      No. You film, pick a look or paste a link, and download a finished Reel. Want a change? Say it in plain words, like "bigger captions" or "keep my intro".

      Are you copying other creators?

      We learn how a video is edited: the cut rhythm, the caption look, how loud the music sits and when the sounds hit. We never use their footage, music or face. Your Reel is 100% your own footage.

      Which links work?

      TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Some Instagram Reels only load for logged-in viewers. If a link won't load, drop in the downloaded video file instead.

      How long does an edit take?

      Minutes, not an evening, and none of them are yours. Close the tab: we email you when it's ready.

      What should I film?

      Hold your phone upright and talk to it, for up to 5 minutes. Retakes are fine. Say the line again and we keep the best one.

      Where does the music come from?

      Every edit gets its own instrumental bed, made for that video and mixed under your voice. Want a trending sound instead? Export without music and add the sound in Instagram or TikTok.

      Is the B-roll stock footage?

      Your own clips come first: add them with your take and we cut them in on the right words. If a line needs a shot you don't have, we make one for that line.
