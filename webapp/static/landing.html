Questions

Frequently asked

Is this AI slop? Can I actually get monetized with these videos?
No, and this is the question we want you to ask. Every recipe is modeled after real channels pulling millions of views and earning real ad revenue. The scripts are original and research-backed, the voiceovers sound natural, and the visuals are purpose-built for each format, not generic AI filler. These are videos you’d normally hire an editor and a scriptwriter to produce. YouTube’s monetization policies require original content with real value to viewers, and that’s exactly what the recipes are designed to deliver. Make a video and judge the output yourself. You can cancel any time.
What’s the best way to start YouTube automation?
Two things: a pre-monetized channel in a proven niche, and a tool that lets you post consistently no matter what. The channel gives you day-one ad revenue so you’re earning from your first upload, with no waiting months to hit 1,000 subscribers. The tool means you can produce a video every single day without burning out or hiring a team. For the channel, head to headstartchannels.com. They sell pre-monetized channels in viral niches, ready to go. For the daily video engine, that’s what ChannelRecipe is. Together, you skip the hardest part of YouTube and go straight to publishing.
What’s the difference between Studio and Classic recipes?
Studio (v2) is the current ChannelRecipe: it runs on your computer, has dozens of styles, clones the style of channels you admire, and adds motion graphics, 3D and a real timeline editor. Classic recipes (v1) are the original quick cloud videos. They’re still included, with monthly credits on every plan.
Can Studio make Shorts?
Yes. Studio has three Shorts recipes: 3D Explainer Shorts, 3D Dark Stories and Photo Comedy Shorts. Type a topic and choose 15 to 60 seconds; Studio writes and fact-checks the script, directs every shot, scores it and cuts a vertical 9:16 video. Your first Short downloads a one-time pack of 3D characters, sets and sounds (about 400 MB). 3D Shorts render on your own computer, so a recent machine is best.
What do I need to use Studio?
A Mac or Windows computer, a Claude account (Pro or Max) for the writing and directing, and a Kie.ai or Atlas Cloud account for voice and images. You pay those providers directly at their own prices. Setup checks everything and tells you exactly what’s missing, and Claude Code can install Studio for you.
Where do I get help?
In our Discord. We answer setup questions there every day, and it’s where we hear what to build next.
Is this YouTube-safe?
Yes. All recipes use original AI-generated content, licensed stock footage, and original scripts. Your channel stays in full compliance with YouTube’s monetization policies.
What if a Classic recipe render fails?
Your credit is automatically refunded. No support ticket needed. You’ll see the credit restored immediately and can try again.
Can I use my own script or voiceover?
Yes. You can edit the AI-generated script freely, or upload your own voiceover recording. The pipeline adapts to whatever you provide.

Your plan (Creator, Pro or Max) bills monthly or yearly from the day you subscribe. Switch plans or cancel anytime from Billing. No hoops, no surprise charges.
